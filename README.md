## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Echoes of Mystralia**. This trainer enhances the single-player Early Access experience by providing optional assistance during exploration, spellcrafting, combat, and roguelite progression. It is designed around the game's core systems, including Memories, spells, Relics, Altar upgrades, Guardian encounters, and permanent Lotus progression.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed way to explore Mystralia, experiment with spell combinations, solve optional challenges, or practice against difficult enemies and bosses.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through the top-down regions of Mystralia and choose routes based on available encounters, rewards, secrets, and challenges. The trainer's gameplay assistance is useful when exploring unfamiliar paths or investigating hidden areas.

### Tools & Inventory

Build each journey around custom spells, Memories, Relics, and Altar upgrades. Memories can be combined, reordered, modified, and enhanced to create different spell effects and combat approaches. Use the trainer to support experimentation rather than compatibility the spellcrafting system.

### World & Environment

Play as Mazarim, a Watcher protecting the Cycle of Memories. Explore regions affected by the Shadows of the Past, meet lost souls, uncover secrets, and investigate hidden puzzles and Rifted Echoes. Each journey can feature different events, encounters, and rewards.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat combines close-range and ranged tactics with freely customized spells. Adapt your spell configuration to enemy groups, Elite fights, Guardians, and bosses. Trainer options can provide survivability during difficult encounters while leaving spell design and tactical decisions to the player.
